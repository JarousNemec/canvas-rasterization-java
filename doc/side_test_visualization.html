Point side test with dot product

Drag point P (and the line endpoints A, B). The sign of n · t tells which side of line AB the point is on.

The purple segment is the projection of t onto n. The dot product equals |n| · (signed length of that projection), so its sign only depends on whether P is on the side n points to. Canvas Y axis points down, which is why (−vy, vx) points to the right of v here and not to the left as in math textbooks.
